Privacy Policy

Effective Date: To be confirmed before public launch

DreavelleVerse respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, and shared when you use our reading and writing platform.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use DreavelleVerse, we may process:

  • Account information, including email address and username.
  • Profile information you choose to provide.
  • Stories, chapters, comments, reviews, and ratings.
  • Reading activity, library selections, bookmarks, likes, follows, and notification preferences.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the website.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to create and manage accounts, display published stories, provide reading and writing features, maintain user preferences, protect accounts, investigate reports, and improve platform reliability.

3. Data Storage and Service Providers

DreavelleVerse uses Supabase for authentication, database services, and related backend functionality. The website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

These providers may process technical or account-related information as necessary to deliver their services.

4. Public and Private Information

Published stories, public profiles, reviews, ratings, and comments may be visible to other users or visitors, depending on the platform's features.

Private reading notes, account credentials, and other account-specific information are intended to be accessible only to authorized users and services.

5. Browser Storage

DreavelleVerse may use browser storage to maintain login sessions, preferences, and website functionality. Supabase authentication may also use browser storage to maintain signed-in sessions.

6. Analytics and Advertising

DreavelleVerse does not currently use Google Analytics or third-party advertising services. This policy will be updated if such services are introduced.

7. Younger Users

DreavelleVerse is intended for users aged 13 and above, subject to applicable local age and consent requirements. Users under 18 should obtain parental or guardian permission where required by law.

Users should not publicly share sensitive personal information, including home addresses, phone numbers, passwords, or school details.

8. Data Security

We use available security controls, including authentication and database access restrictions, to help protect user information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information as needed to operate the platform and meet applicable obligations. Users may request account or personal-data deletion by contacting DreavelleVerse Support.

Deletion requests will be reviewed and handled subject to identity verification, technical requirements, and applicable law.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Users may manage available profile and notification settings. To request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, contact our support email.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, technology, or legal obligations change. Material updates will be communicated where required.
