IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- The age check is self-declared, NOT identity verification or strong age assurance.
- Existing chapter text is served through public Supabase chapter queries; the reader gate is a
  front-end warning, NOT server-enforced access control. A technically capable person could
  bypass it. Do not describe this as secure exclusion of minors.
- Warnings are AUTHOR-MANAGED. The simple text keyword hint is not comprehensive AI moderation.
  Automated server-side detection and review require a separate trusted backend and policies.
- Warnings are stored separately from chapter saves. The writer should explicitly click
  "Save chapter warning" and verify the success message before publishing.
- SQL must be installed before the new features work. Existing likes/follows are not replaced.
- This package is prepared and syntax-checked, but cannot be end-to-end tested against your
  staging Supabase credentials or browser here. Test in staging before any production rollout.
- R18 chapters are not automatically filtered out of search or listings by this update.
